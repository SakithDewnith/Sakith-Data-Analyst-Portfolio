## Customer Retention & Churn Risk Analytics

**Goal:** Identify the customer segments driving the highest revenue loss, uncover the key factors associated with churn, and estimate future revenue exposure to support data-driven retention strategies.

**Code:** [https://github.com/SakithDewnith/Customer-Retention-Churn-Risk-Analytics](https://github.com/SakithDewnith/Customer-Retention-Churn-Risk-Analytics/blob/main/churn%20analysis.ipynb)

**Description:**
The project analyzed a telecom customer dataset containing 7,043 customer records with demographic information, subscription details, contract types, payment methods, monthly charges, and churn status. The analysis involved cleaning and transforming the data using SQL and Python, segmenting customers by contract, tenure, and payment behavior, quantifying churn-related revenue loss, identifying churn drivers within the highest-impact segment, and estimating future revenue exposure among active customers based on historical churn patterns.

**Skills:**
Data Cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Customer Segmentation, Business Analytics, Revenue Analysis, Churn Analysis, Statistical Analysis, Data Visualization.

**Technology:**
SQL, Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib.

**Results:**
The analysis identified Month-to-month customers as the highest-impact segment, accounting for $120.8K in historical monthly revenue loss with a 42.71% churn rate. Customers without Online Security and Tech Support were associated with over $102K each in historical monthly revenue loss, while active customers with these characteristics represented $93K+ and $91K+ in future monthly revenue exposure, providing clear priorities for targeted retention strategies.
